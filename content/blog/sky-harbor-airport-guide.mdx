Phoenix Sky Harbor calls itself America's Friendliest Airport, and by big-hub standards it earns the nickname — but it's still a fifty-million-passenger operation in the middle of a construction boom, and the difference between a smooth trip and a rough one usually comes down to knowing three things: what's changed lately, which terminal you're dealing with, and exactly where your ride is meeting you. Here's the 2026 version of all three.

## What's New at Sky Harbor in 2026

It's been the biggest news year at PHX in a long time.

**Phoenix finally has a nonstop to Asia.** STARLUX Airlines launched Taipei service in January 2026 — the airport's first-ever Asia route — and it worked so well that by July the airline upgraded the route to its flagship A350-1000, including a specially painted aircraft designed with Japanese artist Hajime Sorayama that made its first North American arrival right here.

**Terminal 3 is getting six new gates.** The $326 million North Concourse project is under construction now, adding six gates, a new lounge, new food and drink, and gate-area views of Camelback Mountain. Target completion: 2027. Until then, expect ongoing construction detours around Terminal 3.

**The airport keeps breaking records.** March 2026 was the busiest single month in Sky Harbor history — more than 5.1 million passengers — which is worth knowing if you're flying during spring training or a Final Four weekend: arrive earlier than you think.

**New routes keep landing.** 2026 additions include Porter Airlines' seasonal Vancouver nonstop, new Delta service to Austin, Alaska's daily flights to Sonoma County/Santa Rosa starting November 1, and American's winter seasonal route to Lincoln, Nebraska beginning December 17.

**And under the hood:** a 2,100-foot taxiway bridge connecting the north and south airfields is under construction with completion expected in 2027 — invisible to passengers except in the form of fewer taxi delays once it opens.

## Which Terminal Is My Airline?

Sky Harbor has exactly two passenger terminals — 3 and 4. (Terminal 2 closed in 2020 and was demolished; Terminal 1 has been gone for decades. The numbering is a history lesson, not a map.) The split is simple:

| Terminal | Airlines |
| --- | --- |
| Terminal 4 | American Airlines, Southwest, and most international carriers — international arrivals clear customs here |
| Terminal 3 | Everyone else: Delta, United, Alaska, Frontier, Spirit, Hawaiian, Sun Country, and other domestic carriers |

If you remember one rule: American and Southwest mean Terminal 4, and nearly everything else means Terminal 3. Smaller and newer carriers occasionally move, so glance at your airline's site if you're flying someone outside the majors.

## Where Pickups Actually Happen

Arrivals and baggage claim are on **Level 1** at both terminals, and the doors are numbered — which is the single most useful fact on this page. "I'm outside" means nothing at a terminal with a quarter mile of curb; "Door 8, Level 1, Terminal 4" means your ride finds you in one pass.

Three more things locals know: the curb is enforced, so drivers circling for a late traveler get moved along — anyone waiting for you should sit in the **free cell phone waiting lot** until you're physically at a door. Weekend evenings and event nights (spring training, big games, concert lets-outs) are when the curb crawls and rideshare prices surge with the crowd. ## The PHX Sky Train (Free, 24/7)

Sky Harbor's automated train is genuinely useful and completely free. It runs around the clock connecting the **44th Street station** (where it meets Valley Metro light rail), **East Economy parking**, **Terminal 4**, **Terminal 3**, and the **Rental Car Center**. If you're connecting between terminals, parked in East Economy, or arriving by light rail, the Sky Train is the move — trains come every few minutes and the terminal-to-terminal hop takes about five.

## Sky Harbor FAQ

### Is there a Terminal 1 or Terminal 2 at Sky Harbor?

Not anymore. Terminal 2 closed in 2020 and has been demolished; Terminal 1 is long gone. Every flight uses Terminal 3 or Terminal 4.

### Which terminal is Southwest at Sky Harbor?

Terminal 4 — same as American. Delta, United, Alaska, and most other domestic carriers use Terminal 3.

### How early should I arrive for a flight at PHX?

The standard advice holds: about two hours before a domestic flight, three for international — and add more during peak season (February–April) or major event weekends, when security lines stretch.

### Where does my driver meet me?

Level 1 at your terminal, at a numbered door — or inside at baggage claim if you've booked meet-and-greet. Book with us and your chauffeur texts you the door number and stages to your actual landing time, not your scheduled one.

### Is the cell phone lot free?

Yes — free waiting lots serve both terminals, and that's where anyone picking you up should wait until you're curbside. The terminal curbs are actively enforced.
