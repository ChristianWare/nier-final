You land at Phoenix Sky Harbor and you have three ways to get to your hotel without renting a car: a black car service you booked ahead, Uber Black (or UberX), or a taxi from the curb. All three get you there. They differ on price, how you get picked up, and what happens when your flight is late or it's 5 am. Here's an honest side-by-side, using the most common trip we run: **Sky Harbor to Scottsdale, about 16 miles and 25 minutes**.

## Booked Black Car Service

A black car service means a pre-booked executive sedan or SUV with a professional chauffeur. With Nier:

- **The price is set when you book.** Sky Harbor rides in a black SUV typically run $55–$120 depending on distance, and point-to-point rides start at $55. Traffic, demand and time of night don't change it.
- **Your flight is tracked.** If you land early, your chauffeur is already there. If you're delayed, we adjust at no extra charge.
- **You choose how to meet.** Meet & greet puts your chauffeur inside baggage claim with a name sign. Curbside means we text when you land and pull up when you walk out.
- **Early flights are confirmed ahead.** The 4 am and 5 am departures that app drivers often decline are confirmed the night before, with a chauffeur assigned in advance.

**Best for:** business travelers, families with luggage, early departures, groups, and anyone who wants to know the price and the pickup before they get on the plane.

## Uber Black

Uber Black is Uber's premium tier: newer black cars and higher-rated drivers, requested in the app when you land.

- **Price:** Uber lists an average of **$84 for Uber Black** and **$113 for Black SUV** from PHX to Scottsdale, with UberX averaging $42. Prices move with demand, so a busy Friday afternoon or a big event week can cost more.
- **Pickup:** you walk to the airport's designated rideshare pickup area, then wait for your car.
- **Availability:** usually good in the middle of the day. Less predictable for very early mornings, late-night arrivals and big event weeks.

**Best for:** solo travelers with a carry-on, at a normal hour, who don't mind a little uncertainty on price and wait.

## Taxi

Taxis at Sky Harbor charge set rates no matter which company you get: **$7 for the first mile and $2.60 for each additional mile**, plus **$36 per hour of traffic delay**, with an **$18 minimum**. There's also a **$20 flat rate** from the airport to central downtown Phoenix. For the 16 miles to Scottsdale, that works out to about $46 before traffic and tip.

- **Pickup:** at the taxi stand. In Terminal 3, that's the north curb outside door 7. In Terminal 4, it's Level 1, at the north curb outside door 7 or the south curb outside door 4.
- **Vehicle:** whatever's next in line.

**Best for:** quick trips to downtown Phoenix on the flat rate, or when you didn't book anything ahead.

## Which Should You Book?

- **Book a black car** if you have an early flight, a group, a lot of luggage, a client to impress, or you just want the price and the pickup settled before you fly. In a black SUV, it's often in the same range as Uber Black, and the price doesn't move.
- **Take Uber Black or UberX** if you're traveling light at a normal hour and want to decide when you land.
- **Take a taxi** for a short hop downtown on the $20 flat rate.

## Sky Harbor Ride FAQ

### Is a black car service more expensive than Uber Black?

Often not. From Sky Harbor to Scottsdale, Uber lists an average of $84 for Uber Black and $113 for Black SUV, while a Nier black SUV typically runs $55–$120 depending on distance, with the price set when you book.

### How much is a taxi from Sky Harbor to Scottsdale?

About $46 for the 16 miles at Sky Harbor's posted rates ($7 for the first mile, $2.60 for each additional mile), plus $36 per hour of traffic delay and any tip.

### Where do taxis pick up at Sky Harbor?

In Terminal 3, at the north curb outside door 7. In Terminal 4, on Level 1 at the north curb outside door 7 or the south curb outside door 4.

### What happens if my flight is delayed?

With a booked black car, we track your flight and adjust the pickup at no extra charge. With an app or a taxi, you request a ride or wait in line when you land.

### Can a black car fit my whole group?

Yes. Sedans fit up to 3 passengers, SUVs fit small groups with luggage, and executive Sprinters carry up to 14.
