## How Charter Bus Pricing Works

A charter bus is priced on **time, not seats**. You're renting the whole bus and driver for your group, so the main things that set the price are:

1. **The bus size.** A Sprinter, a mini bus and a full-size coach each have their own hourly rate.
2. **The hours.** Pickup to final drop-off, with a 2-hour minimum. Waiting time in the middle counts, because the bus and driver stay with you.
3. **The route.** Extra stops and out-of-town miles add time. Day trips to Sedona or Tucson are usually booked as a full day.
4. **The date.** Event weeks book up first (see below), so the earlier you book, the better your chances of getting the bus you want.

## Sample Charter Trips and Prices

These are typical ranges, not quotes, but they show how the totals usually come together:

- **Sky Harbor group transfer (40 people, one way):** a mini bus or coach for the 2-hour minimum. Often the lowest-cost way to move a conference group, usually toward the low end of each range.
- **Wedding guest shuttle (50 guests, 4–5 hours):** a motor coach running loops between the hotel, ceremony and reception. Typically about **$1,000–$1,500**.
- **Corporate off-site (25 people, half day):** a 28-passenger mini bus from the office to the venue and back. Typically about **$675–$1,400** depending on the hours.
- **Sedona day trip (35 people):** a 40-passenger mini bus or coach for a full day, about 2 hours each way. Priced by the day's total hours, so it lands at the high end or above.
- **Multi-day conference shuttle:** several days of hotel-to-venue loops, booked as one trip with one invoice. Each day is priced on its own hours.

## What Does a Charter Bus Cost Per Person?

Split across a full bus, a charter is often cheaper per person than you'd expect:

- **$1,200 coach for 50 people:** about **$24 per person**
- **$1,000 mini bus for 40 people:** about **$25 per person**
- **$675 mini bus for 25 people:** about **$27 per person**

Compare that with a round of rideshares or a dozen cars paying for event parking, plus the time it takes to get everyone back together, and the bus usually wins for any group over 15.

## How to Keep the Total Down

- **Pick the bus by headcount.** A 28-passenger mini bus costs less than a coach running half empty. Our [group transportation guide](/services/group-transportation) shows which bus fits which group size.
- **Plan the hours honestly.** Map pickup to final drop-off, then add a small buffer so you don't run over.
- **Book the whole trip at once.** Multi-day events and round trips go on one booking, which is easier to plan and price.
- **Book early for event weeks.** You'll get the bus size you actually want instead of what's left.

## Charter Bus Cost FAQ

### How much does it cost to rent a charter bus in Phoenix?

Recent charters with us have typically come to about $1,000–$1,500 for a 56-passenger motor coach, about $675–$1,400 for a 28- or 40-passenger mini bus, and about $360–$1,300 for an executive Sprinter, with a 2-hour minimum.

### Is a charter bus priced per hour or per person?

Per hour, for the whole bus. You rent the bus and driver for your group, so the price doesn't change with how many seats you fill.

### What's the minimum rental for a charter bus?

2 hours. Most airport transfers and short event runs fit in that minimum.

### How much is a charter bus to Sedona or the Grand Canyon?

Day trips are priced by the total hours. Sedona is about 2 hours each way from Phoenix, so most groups book a full day. The Grand Canyon is about 3½–4 hours each way, and federal driver-hour limits can mean a second driver or an overnight, which we plan when we quote.

### Is the tip included?

No. The driver tip is optional.

### Can we get more than one bus?

Yes. For conventions, tournaments and big weddings we send as many coaches and mini buses as you need, on one booking with one coordinator and one invoice. Planning in Scottsdale? See **[charter bus rental in Scottsdale](/charter-bus-rental-scottsdale)**.
