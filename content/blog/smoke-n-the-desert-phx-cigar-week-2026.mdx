Smoke 'N The Desert is back for 2026, and **PHX Cigar Week runs Thursday, October 8 through Sunday, October 11** — four days of premium cigars, live music, nightlife and networking spread across the Valley. The organizers sum it up as "4 Days, 10 Events, 1 Helluva Weekend," and the weekend centers on Saturday's outdoor Cigar & Music Festival in [Downtown Chandler](/locations/chandler). Cigar aficionados, first-timers, professionals and visitors flying in from around the country all end up in the same lounges by Sunday. Here's the schedule, what to know about tickets, and the part that's easy to overlook when your weekend spans six cities: how you'll get from one event to the next.

## When Is Smoke 'N The Desert 2026?

October 8–11, 2026. Events run all four days in [Phoenix](/locations/phoenix), [Tempe](/locations/tempe), [Mesa](/locations/mesa), Chandler, [Scottsdale](/locations/scottsdale) and [Glendale](/locations/glendale), with the signature festival on Saturday, October 10, starting at 2 pm. Lineups like this can shift, so check the official schedule at [smokenthedesert.com](https://www.smokenthedesert.com) before you lock in plans.

## The Saturday Festival in Downtown Chandler

The main event is the **Smoke 'N The Desert Cigar & Music Festival on Saturday, October 10, at Dr. A.J. Chandler Park East**, right in the middle of Downtown Chandler. It's an outdoor, afternoon-into-evening festival with premium cigar brands, live music and entertainment, food and drinks, and lifestyle vendors, and it's where the whole PHX Cigar Week community comes together in one place. Two things to know before you go: **the festival is 21+, and tickets are required.**

Downtown Chandler is a great setting for it — restaurants, bars and the Hilton Garden Inn Downtown Chandler are all within a few blocks of the park — but on festival day, the nearby parking and the streets around the park fill up fast.

## Tickets, Passes and Where to Stay

Weekend passes are sold through the official site in two tiers: **General Admission**, which covers the weekend's general events, and **VIP**, with elevated access across the weekend. Some events are ticketed on their own, so check each one on the schedule. Prices and availability change as the weekend approaches, so buy directly from [smokenthedesert.com](https://www.smokenthedesert.com) rather than at the door.

Coming in from out of town? The event has a group room block at the **Hilton Garden Inn Downtown Chandler** — mention Smoke 'N The Desert when you book. It puts you a short walk from the festival and close to several of the weekend's official experiences.

## Getting Around a Six-City Weekend

Here's the honest part. Smoke 'N The Desert doesn't happen in one venue — it moves. Thursday starts in Downtown Phoenix and ends in Tempe or Ahwatukee. Friday goes from a Mesa lounge to a Tempe rooftop that runs until 1 am. Saturday is Mesa in the morning and Chandler all afternoon and night. Sunday splits between Scottsdale and Glendale, which sit on opposite sides of the Valley. Add a weekend of cigars and cocktails, and driving yourself stops making sense by Thursday night.
