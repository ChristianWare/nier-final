## How Much Does It Cost to Rent a Limo in Phoenix?

Limo rental is priced **by the hour with a 2-hour minimum**. That's the most important thing to know when you compare quotes, because the hourly rate alone doesn't tell you the total. A few hours for a wedding, a prom night or an anniversary dinner covers most bookings.

To estimate your total:

1. **Map out your timeline.** Start when you need to be picked up and end when the last person gets dropped off.
2. **Add a buffer.** Photos run long and dinners run late. Add 30–60 minutes so you're not paying overtime.
3. **Multiply by the hourly rate** for the vehicle you choose. Our booking tool does this for you and shows the total before you confirm.

**How much to rent a limo for a day?** Same math. A full wedding day is often 6–8 hours from the getting-ready pickup to the grand exit, so it's worth asking whether two shorter bookings (one before the ceremony, one for the exit) cost less than holding the limo all day.

## How Much Is a Black Car Service in Phoenix?

Black car service is the everyday version: a pre-booked executive sedan or SUV with a professional chauffeur.

- **Point-to-point rides start at $55.** That's our minimum fare for any one-way trip.
- **Sky Harbor rides in a black SUV typically run $55–$120**, depending on how far you're going. Scottsdale, Paradise Valley and the East Valley are common runs.
- **Hourly chauffeur service has a 2-hour minimum.** Your chauffeur stays with you between stops, which is how executives handle a day of meetings or a night in Old Town.

## How Much Does a Party Bus Cost in Phoenix?

Party buses are booked by the hour with a 2-hour minimum. A typical mini party bus night for 20–40 people runs **about $675–$1,400**, depending on the hours, the night of the week and your route. Split across a full bus, that's often less per person than a round of rideshares, with nobody stuck driving. See our **[party bus rentals](/services/party-bus)** for what's on board.

## How Much Does a Charter Bus Cost in Phoenix?

A full-size motor coach for up to 56 passengers is the most cost-effective way to move a big group. Typical charters run **about $1,000–$1,500**, with a 2-hour minimum. Corporate shuttles, wedding guest loops, game days and day trips to Sedona or Tucson are the most common bookings. Details are on our **[charter bus rental](/charter-bus-rental-phoenix)** page.

## What Affects the Price?

- **Hours.** Hourly bookings bill at least the 2-hour minimum. Book what you need, plus a buffer.
- **Distance.** Flat-rate trips are priced by the route, so a North Scottsdale pickup costs more than a downtown one.
- **Vehicle.** A sedan, a Sprinter and a motor coach are priced very differently, and so are one vehicle and two.
- **Date and time.** Prom season, wedding Saturdays, the WM Phoenix Open and the Fiesta Bowl book up first. Booking early gets you the vehicle you want.
- **Extras.** Extra stops ($15 each) and child seats can be added when you book. Meet & greet inside baggage claim is free on airport pickups.

## What's Included?

Your quote covers the vehicle, a uniformed, background-checked chauffeur, and the trip as booked. Airport pickups include flight tracking, so a delay doesn't cost you anything extra, plus up to 60 minutes of free wait time after you land. Free cancellation up to 72 hours before pickup. The driver tip is optional and never built in. For larger bookings, you can often secure the date with a deposit and pay the balance later.
